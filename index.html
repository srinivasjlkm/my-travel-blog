A Side Note

What You See Is What Was There

Every photo on this site is uploaded straight off the camera — no heavy saturation, no sky swaps, no pushing the water to a colour it never actually was. You'll find edited versions of some of these same shots over on my Instagram, and there's nothing wrong with that — I just wanted this blog specifically to stay the unedited, real-colour version. Water and skies exactly as they looked when I was standing in front of them.
