Jeju

7 Days · 2019

Cool, rainy weather the whole trip, and somehow still one of the most scenic weeks of the entire run — waterfalls, cliffs, tea fields, freediving grandmothers, and a genuinely wild range of quirky museums packed onto one island. The standout was Udo Island — a ferry out from Seongsanpo Port, then a full day cycling the coastline, capped off with the famous peanut ice cream before the evening ferry back. We also caught local haenyeo (women freedivers) fishing just offshore, which was something else to watch in person.

→ Flew in as a group of four (Malaysian, Filipino, and Indian passports) — Jeju was visa-free for all of us at the time. Rented a car for the week; a Malaysian driving licence was enough to drive locally, no international permit needed.
