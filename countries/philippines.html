The Story

7,000+ islands and I've barely made a dent. These are the routes I keep coming back to — and sending friends on. Everything here is backpacker style — budget guesthouses over resorts, more time out on the water and in town than sitting in a hotel room.

🧭 To keep navigation simple, I just searched the name of each place in Google Maps and followed the route — never had real difficulties doing it this way. That said, don't expect perfect roads everywhere; some destinations have a much rougher path than others.

Where I Went

Manila

5 Days · Dec 2018

The sprawling starting point — colonial Intramuros, chaotic jeepneys, and a food scene that genuinely never sleeps.

→ A 4-hour flight in from Malaysia to kick off the trip.

CityFood

Sagada

3 Days · Dec 2018

Mountain air, hanging coffins carved into limestone cliffs, and rice terraces that appear as the morning fog lifts.

→ About 6 hours by bus from Manila, through Baguio.

MountainsBus RideNature

Palawan

4 Days · Dec 2018

The Philippines' 'Last Frontier' — even the well-known corners still feel a little undiscovered.

Islands

Coron

4 Days

Impossibly clear lagoons ringed by limestone karsts, with WWII shipwrecks resting in turquoise water just offshore.

→ A straightforward 2-hour flight from Manila.

DivingLagoons

Batangas

5 Days · 2024

Two very different sides of the same province — a quiet freediving base and a long stretch of open beach, both easy trips from Manila.

BudgetWeekend Trip

Mabini

2 Days

A quiet freediving base on the Verde Island Passage — one of the most biodiverse stretches of water on the planet.

→ A 2-hour drive from Tagaytay.

Freediving

Calatagan

3 Days

Long stretches of pale sand and calm water — a low-key coastal escape without the crowds.

→ Details coming soon.

Beach

Bohol

5 Days · 2019 & 2026

Two visits, two very different sides — resort beaches in Panglao, and the quieter blue holes of Anda.

Island

Panglao

4 Days · Summer 2019

White sand beaches by day, and the otherworldly Chocolate Hills rolling across the interior.

→ About a 1-hour flight from Manila to Tagbilaran.

BeachNature

Anda

1 Day · 2026

Bohol's quieter side — untouched beaches and blue holes, without Panglao's resort crowds.

→ A 2-hour ferry from Siquijor into Tagbilaran, then a trike and bus out to Anda — mostly used as a stop on the way to Camiguin this time. We also rented a motorbike and drove up to Chocolate Hills — about a 2-hour ride from Jagna Port.

BeachTransit Stop

Thousands of grass-covered mounds that turn cocoa-brown in the dry season, seen here from the lookout point above them.

Bicol

4 Days · Dec 2023

Mayon Volcano's near-perfect cone watches over the road the whole way, with some of the country's spiciest food waiting at the end of it.

→ A 2-day motorbike ride from Manila, with overnight stops in Pagbilao and Naga City.

MotorbikeVolcanoFood

Romblon

4 Days · 2025

The 'Marble Capital of the Philippines' — quiet fishing towns, marble quarries, and coastline that barely sees a tourist.

→ About a 6-hour ferry from Batangas.

IslandOff the Beaten Path

Sibuyan

4 Days · 2025

Known as the 'Galapagos of Asia' — dense rainforest, rivers running gin-clear, and Mt. Guiting-Guiting looming over all of it.

→ A 4-hour ferry from Romblon.

RainforestFerry

Siquijor

5 Days · 2026

An island wrapped in old mysticism, with waterfalls the colour of glass tucked into the jungle.

→ Flight from Manila to Dumaguete (1 hour), a quick trike to the port, then a short ferry over to the island. We rented a motorbike right at the port for all 5 days on the island — there are plenty of rental stalls there and you don't need to book ahead. I pre-booked with 'Bibings Car & Motorbike Rentals' (found them on Facebook) since I wanted a specific type of bike — their shop is right next to the port too, and they gave a good discount for renting more than 2 days.

WaterfallsIsland

A multi-level waterfall you can swim and jump between — go early, since it gets crowded with tour groups after around noon.

Camiguin

4 Days · 2026

More volcanoes than towns, with two very different landmarks worth the trip.

→ A 4-hour ferry from Jagna, Bohol. Getting back out: ferry to Balingoan, bus to Cagayan de Oro, overnight, then a flight home to Manila. We rented a motorbike from where we stayed, Marianita's Cottages, for all 4 days on the island.

BeachIsland

A pure white sandbar sitting in open water off the coast, with Camiguin's volcanoes as a backdrop — no shade, no structures, just sand and sea for as far as the tide allows.

A cemetery swallowed by the sea after a volcanic eruption in 1871, marked today by a large cross rising out of the water just offshore — you can swim, snorkel, or take a short boat out to it.
