City

Saigon

5 Days · Solo trip

Also known as Ho Chi Minh City. Stayed in capsule hotels here, which were pretty comfortable and affordable. Out in Mui Ne, rented a scooter and drove around the coastal roads — scenic desert sand dunes on one side, clear water on the other — plus some sand dune activities and an ATV ride, staying at a small backpacker place in town for two days. Back in Saigon, found a scooter tour company called Kiss Tours, and they were fantastic — you just tell them your mood and what you feel like seeing or doing, and they build the route around that. Great nightlife and bar scene if that's your thing, genuinely kind and friendly people throughout, and I checked out a few museums and walked around the city on foot, which felt pretty safe the whole time.

→ Flew in from Penang — this one was a solo trip. Took a bus out to Mui Ne early on, a proper overnight sleeper bus experience, then came back to Saigon for the rest of the trip. Traffic in the city is heavy enough that I chose not to rent and drive myself here, unlike Mui Ne.
