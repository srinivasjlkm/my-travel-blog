About

Hi, I'm Srinivas

I started travelling in 2019 and haven't really stopped. This journal exists for two reasons: to put my photos somewhere they can actually breathe, past the 4:5 crop of Instagram — and to write down the practical side of every trip, the flights, the ferries, the rented scooters, before I forget the details.

If a photo here makes you want to book a flight, that's the whole point. And if the logistics pages save you from overpaying for a boat transfer, even better.

Everything here is real: real routes, real costs (in the currency and year I travelled), real recommendations. Nothing sponsored unless it's labelled.
