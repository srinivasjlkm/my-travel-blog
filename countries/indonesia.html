Bali

TBD · 2017 (approx)

Took a boat over to Nusa Penida from Sanur Beach for a day trip — the crossing gets genuinely rough, so it's worth being mindful if you're prone to seasickness. We really wished we could've stayed longer than the one day we had there. Sanur Beach itself is beautiful for sunrise, and Tanah Lot is one of the best spots on the island for sunset. Uluwatu Temple was a highlight for a different reason — the cultural dance and storytelling performances there (kecak dance) were incredible, though it's worth keeping a close eye on your belongings, since there are a lot of monkeys around who have no problem helping themselves. With the rest of the trip on the rented van, we made it out to the Ubud rice terraces, Pantai Pandawa, and Nusa Dua Beach as well. This was back around 2017 — Bali's become a lot more crowded since, so if you're planning something similar now, better planning ahead is worth it.

→ Went with a group of friends — mostly got around in a rented van, like a mini van, which worked out cheaper split across the group for both van hire and boat trip packages.
